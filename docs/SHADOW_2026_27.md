# SHADOW CANDIDATES, 2026-27 — pre-registered before any 2026-27 price exists

Frozen 2026-08-09. The production rule (offset margin, walk-forward threshold,
D232 absence term) is FROZEN for the season; these eight candidates run as
shadow logs beside it and none may place or size a bet. Every constant below is
fixed now, deliberately round, so nothing can be tuned once data arrives.

Why these: D239 established the signal is RANKABLE (signed opener residual
and signed CLV both rise in |stated edge|, CIs excluding zero, K=7) and that no
observable STATE modifies that trust (0/3 continuous interactions, after 0/3
bucket tests in D237/D237b). So the open question is not "which games" but
"which FORECASTS" — forecast-specific reliability and information freshness,
which need live infrastructure and could not be tested historically.

## C1 — Uncertainty-adjusted edge (lower confidence bound)

At decision time, rebuild the margin under availability scenarios: every player
with 0.15 < P(out) < 0.85 is flipped fully-in and fully-out (one at a time;
cap 8 players/game by |talent x minutes|). Let sigma_hat = sd of the resulting
margins and flip_frac = share of scenarios that change the bet side.

    RobustEdge = |d| - 1.0 * sigma_hat - 0.25          (points)
    shadow-bet iff RobustEdge > threshold AND flip_frac <= 0.15

q = 1.0, buffer = 0.25 pts, flip cap 15%: structural constants, chosen for
roundness before any data. WHY LIVE-ONLY: D198 found fitted outcome-variance
nearly flat in every T0/T1 input (~0.5% per SD), so historical per-game sigma
carries no usable variation; scenario dispersion at decision time is the first
game-specific sigma this project can compute.

## C2 — Post-open information shock

Decompose the disagreement at decision time t:

    d(t) = [M(t_open) - O] + [M(t) - M(t_open)]

using the logger's snapshot ladder for O and the model recomputed at each
snapshot (the P(out) artifact updates as report editions arrive — which is why
the every-edition fetch in OCTOBER_CAPTURE_SPEC is a prerequisite).

    shadow-bet iff |M(t) - M(t_open)| >= 0.5 pts
                AND the market has moved < 50% of that shock since the open

Mechanism: bet when timestamp-verifiable news moved the model and the market
has not yet caught up. Both constants fixed now.

## C3 — Perturbation robustness + price stress

    shadow-bet iff the SAME side survives >= 85% of the C1 scenario set
                AND the edge remains > threshold after a 0.5-pt line stress

## C0 — Tail hinge (added after D239b, at the reviewer's instruction)

The "signal only in the tail" reading is POST-SELECTION: D1-D8 were pooled after
their results were seen and the breakpoint located near 1.2 pts by eye. The
clean historical evidence is the continuous positive slope, not the hinge. So
the hinge is fixed NOW and tested prospectively:

    logit P(cover) = a + b1|e| + b2 (|e| - 1.25)+          tau = 1.25 pts, FROZEN

Primary endpoint signed CLV, secondary cover. tau is not re-estimated on
history; if it is ever re-fitted it must be inside each training fold and
applied unchanged forward.

## C4 — Paired D232 absence test

Log BOTH forecasts every game: frozen pre-D232 blind margin and the shipped
production margin. Pre-registered paired endpoints: (1) signed-CLV improvement
from the correction; (2) reduction in the depleted-side overbetting rate;
(3) edge calibration in absence-heavy games; (4) no degradation on healthy
rosters. This is the cleanest causal-style test available, because the
correction was designed and shipped BEFORE the subgroup result that motivates
the test was ever looked at.

## C5 — Regularised total-conditioned margin scale (D243, frozen here)

D241 tested an UNRESTRICTED total-conditioned scale and it was inconclusive:
s1 > 0 in all six folds (the mechanism), but better in only 2/6, CI straddling
zero, and the coefficient decayed 0.236 -> 0.036 as training grew. The
historical folds are now spent, so a shrinkage grid on them would be a
second-look optimisation rather than confirmation.

ONE specification, frozen now, prospective only:

    sigma_i = sigma_0 * (T_i / Tbar)^gamma        gamma penalised toward 0
    penalty  ridge on gamma with lambda = 50, FIXED HERE, not selected

Required arms, all four scored: constant scale · this regularised form ·
D241's unrestricted form (diagnostic reproduction) · total SHUFFLED within
season-and-spread buckets (placebo).

**Acceptance requires a minimum practically-relevant effect, not just
significance.** D241 declared no such threshold, which in hindsight was the gap
that made "statistically nonzero" the implicit bar for a variance term. Here:
full-stack log-loss improvement >= 0.0005 nats with the CI entirely below zero,
no single fold contributing most of it, stable calibration slope, and a
coefficient of sensible sign and magnitude.

Priority: BELOW the joint-minutes and role-transition work. This is a small
effect at best and it is queued last deliberately.

## C6 — Candidate-coverage guard on the minute allocator (D245c, frozen here)

D245c measured that the rank-constrained allocator's benefit is STRONGEST when
the candidate pool is complete and turns HARMFUL at L1 when it is not:

    OTHER == 0  (n=5,669)   tier benefit dTV **-0.00959**
    OTHER >  0  (n=8,610)   tier benefit dTV **+0.00304**
    slope of dTV on OTHER share  +0.06556

**THE CONSTRAINT THAT SHAPES THIS GUARD: realised OTHER share is NOT KNOWABLE
AT THE OPENER.** It depends on who actually plays. So the guard cannot threshold
on the quantity that was measured — it needs an EX-ANTE predictor of coverage.

**AND A HARD CUTOFF WOULD BE SELECTION.** Any specific value picked now would be
chosen with knowledge of these six seasons. The frozen construction therefore
uses NO THRESHOLD AT ALL:

    c_hat_g = P(candidate pool covers the roster | opener-time features)
    weights = c_hat_g * allocator + (1 - c_hat_g) * control

A continuous blend, so there is nothing to tune and no cutoff to select. `c_hat`
is fitted walk-forward on prior seasons only, against the realised coverage
label, using ONLY opener-time features:

  * candidate count
  * days since each rostered player last appeared (max, and count > 12)
  * players on the injury report with a long consecutive-Out run
  * team games played in the last 14 days
  * days since the team's last roster transaction, where available
  * early-season flag (days into season)

**FALLBACK IS THE CONTROL, NEVER A ZERO.** If `c_hat` cannot be computed the
weight is 0, i.e. pure control — the allocator is opt-in, so a broken coverage
model costs the improvement rather than the prediction.

Reported alongside every result: realised coverage, `c_hat` calibration
(predicted vs realised coverage decile), and the share of games where the blend
weight fell below 0.5.

## C7 — Movement-teacher timing signal (D246b, frozen here)

D246b found that a ridge on seven opener-time features predicts open-to-close
movement with **+0.1299 OOS R^2**, and that betting the predicted-movement side
improves signed CLV over the shipped offset by **+0.1117 pts, CI [+0.0528,
+0.1706], 6/6 seasons** — while outcome log loss stays null. That is a TIMING
signal, not a better forecast.

Frozen: the seven features (edge, |open|, rest differential, expected-absence
differential, total, days into season, trailing market log loss), standardised
on training folds, ridge lambda = 50 in standardised space, intercept
unpenalised. Target `close - open`. **The close is a TRAINING TARGET ONLY** —
the leakage check that permutes it within season and requires bit-identical
features is part of the frozen spec.

**THE FROZEN POLICY — corrected in D246c, because C7's historical evidence came
from `sign(pM)` as a SIDE rule, which is not what is being frozen:**

    baseline_side = the frozen production side          (C7 may NEVER flip it)
    z = baseline_side * predicted(close - open)          threshold exactly 0
    z >  0  -> execute the baseline side at the current quote
    z <= 0  -> wait; the line is predicted to improve

Evidence for this policy, side held fixed: CLV(z>0) - CLV(z<=0) = **+1.0047,
CI [+0.6923, +1.3172], 6/6 seasons**, retention 74.5%.

**WHAT "WAIT" MEANS — fully specified now, because D246d showed the historical
test contained NO DELAYED FILL and therefore measured group separation rather
than execution gain:**

    on z <= 0, do NOT execute at the decision timestamp. Re-evaluate at each
    subsequent ladder snapshot (T-4h, T-2h, T-1h). Execute at the FIRST
    snapshot where z > 0. If none occurs, execute MANDATORILY at the T-15m
    close snapshot. There is no cancellation and no lost bet.

**THE PAIRED ESTIMATOR.** Record the executable quote L_0 at the decision
timestamp and L_C7 at whatever snapshot C7 actually fills. Then

    delta_exec = s*(C - L_C7) - s*(C - L_0) = s*(L_0 - L_C7)

The close CANCELS, so this measures price improvement directly and needs no
outcome. It is PAIRED WITHIN GAME, so |edge|-decile matching is a secondary
robustness check, not the primary estimator.

**DENOMINATORS.** Every baseline opportunity is in the denominator; a missed or
unavailable fill is never discarded. Report separately: immediate-execution
rate, eventual-fill rate, lost-bet rate.

**SCALE EXPECTATION, SET NOW.** On the historical ledger only 12.2% of bets
would defer and the illustrative benefit is ~0.03 points of CLV per bet. C7 is
a small execution refinement, not a source of alpha, and should be judged
against that scale rather than against the +1.42 group separation.

**MONITOR:** the zero-threshold action rate drifted 62.3% -> 79.5% across the
historical sample. Track it live. The threshold stays at exactly zero.

Discovered on spent seasons, so historical significance does not promote it.

## Endpoint and gate (one gate for all three)

Primary endpoint: INCREMENTAL SIGNED CLV of shadow-selected bets versus
baseline bets MATCHED ON |d| DECILE — the matching is what stops a candidate
"winning" by simply retaining larger edges (D239's slope makes that
otherwise-guaranteed). Secondary: ATS return sign.

A candidate is promoted to a 2027-28 sizing experiment only if ALL of:
  1. incremental signed CLV one-sided 95% lower bound > 0, clustered by week;
  2. the increment exceeds the measured quote-synchronisation error
     (computed from the logger's own timestamps; a +0.03-pt "edge" under a
     0.10-pt sync error is nothing);
  3. positive in >= 4 of 6 alternating in-season blocks;
  4. retains >= 25% of baseline opportunities;
  5. no single month supplies > 50% of the effect;
  6. ATS increment has the same sign (significance not required);
  7. zero data-integrity violations (a missing/stale feed is a NO-PREDICTION
     state, never a zero — missingness is a state, not a value).

Mid-season retuning of any constant voids the candidate for the season.

## Sample size, corrected

D238 reported "~45 bets to confirm CLV > 0". That assumed independent bets and
took the observed +1.07 as the true forward effect. Both are wrong. D239b
measures a WEEKLY design effect of 2.91, and a winner's-curse shrink is
mandatory on an effect discovered in the same data:

    no shrinkage        n ~ 131 bets
    30% shrink          n ~ 267 bets
    50% shrink          n ~ 523 bets

At ~127 bets/season on one book, confirming CLV is a ONE-TO-TWO SEASON exercise,
not a half-season one. Every gate above inherits that horizon.
