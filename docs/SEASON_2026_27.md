# 2026-27 season, out of sample (through 2026-11-04)

The first 121 regular-season games of 2026-27, October 20 to November 4. Every
coefficient scored here was fit only on seasons before this one. Not every rule was
fixed before opening night, and the table below says which. **This is a replay, not a
live betting record**, for the reason given further down.

## What was fixed in advance and what was not

| Piece | Fit on | Fixed | Had it seen 2026-27 games? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moneyline model: market-blind margin plus a correction to the line | through 2025-26 | before opening night | No |
| The four moneyline betting rules and their cap | 2022-26 | 2026-08-01 | No |
| Injury-status probabilities | 2018-26 | 2026-11-05 | No |
| Default rating for a rookie with no rating yet | debuts 2017-26 | 2026-11-05 | No |
| Player points engine | through 2025-26 | before opening night | No |
| Totals model and its 2-point rule | 2007-26 | 2026-11-05 | No |
| Stake in proportion to the gap | 2012-26 backtest | 2026-11-05 | No |
| Day-before spread rule: its time window and threshold | not fitted | 2026-11-05 | **Yes** |

So the moneyline rows are a pre-season model scored on a new season. The totals rows are
a model built during the season from earlier seasons only, then run once on this one. The
day-before spread rule was designed after looking at these same games and is in-sample.
Player ratings come from an outside provider (DARKO) that revises its past values, and
the values used here were fetched on 2026-11-05, so they may carry some hindsight.

## Summary

- **Forecast accuracy (log loss): no edge over the market yet.** The shipped model trails
  the moneyline it is anchored on at every time of day, by amounts too small to be
  significant at 121 games.
- **Selection strategy: 27-13 at opening lines, break-even at game-day lines.** Betting
  only the games where the model disagrees with the line by 2+ points went 5-0 against
  the spread and 22-13 on totals when priced at the opening number. Priced at the first
  total our own poller saw on game day, totals went 14-13.
- **On the games it bets, the model's log loss is better than the market's in every
  frame.** On the games it passes, the model is worse. None of these differences is
  significant yet.
- **Player points: not ready.** On 333 real lines the engine scored worse than the line,
  because it is overconfident. Its strongest picks did well, but not significantly.
- **Rookies: the data is now complete, but two weeks of it is thin.** All 53 rookies who
  have played have a rating. In past seasons a rookie's two-week rating said little about
  where he finished.
- **The sample is small.** Forty bets cannot separate skill from luck, and the opening
  prices were not captured live.

## Why this is a replay

The live paper book ran from opening night, but an audit on 2026-11-05 found that it was
not running the system the backtests describe:

- The injury-report poller looked for a file name the league stopped using in January
  2026 and captured nothing all season.
- Player ratings were frozen at their July values, and every rookie was priced as a
  league-average player.
- Players who had left a team years ago were counted as absent from it, which also made
  one betting rule fire on absences that were not real.
- Jobs were scheduled in the wrong time zone, so two of the three paper views priced
  games after they had tipped.
- The closing price used for line-value scoring was the entry price itself.

All of these are fixed. The numbers below re-price each game with the corrected code,
using only what was published before the price it is compared with: tables as of that
morning, and injury reports only if stamped before the quote. No coefficient was refit.
The live path as it actually ran is shown once, in the first table, so the cost of the
bugs is on the record.

## Forecast accuracy

Log loss, lower is better. A coin flip scores 0.6931.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10am on game day | 0.5769 | 0.5704 | +0.0065 (-0.0052, +0.0187) | 68.6% / 68.6% |

| 25 minutes before tip | 0.5695 | 0.5648 | +0.0046 (-0.0062, +0.0158) | 67.8% / 69.4% |

- Closing spread, nine books: 0.5625. Closing moneyline, DraftKings: 0.5648.

The shipped model is the market line plus about a third of the market-blind model's
disagreement with it. A positive difference means the model was worse.

### Spread, first line posted the day before, 111 games

The first snapshot with a spread from two or more books, between 5pm the day before and
12 hours before tip. Median 25 hours before tip, 6 books.

| Forecast | Log loss (home win) | Mean absolute error, points |
|---|---|---|
| First line | 0.5622 | 11.11 |
| First line + model correction | 0.5624 | 11.05 |
| Line on game-day morning | 0.5574 | not computed |
| Closing line | 0.5502 | 11.04 |

The model's correction does not improve log loss on the first line (+0.0003, CI -0.0127
to +0.0130). What it does do is point the right way: the line later moved toward the
model by 27% of the disagreement (± 5), and about 70% of that move had happened by
game-day morning.

### Totals, 121 games

A totals model was added this season. It predicts how far the final total will land from
the opening number, from nine things known when the total opens: mostly how the market
has recently priced these two teams and the league. It was fit on 2007-08 to 2025-26 and
frozen. In the pre-season walk-forward (2012-26) its over/under picks at a 2+ point gap
covered 55.6% on 2,449 bets.

| Measured against | Squared error: line / model / close | Over-under log loss: model vs 0.6931 (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| DraftKings' opening total | 360.3 / 355.7 / 341.2 | 0.6825, -0.0107 (-0.0241, +0.0030) |
| First total our poller saw (game day) | 349.2 / 351.5 / 341.2 | 0.6906, -0.0025 (-0.0148, +0.0097) |

Over-under log loss treats the model's predicted gap as a probability of the over, with
the spread of outcomes fixed at 18 points from pre-season data. The line itself implies
50%, which is the 0.6931 baseline.

## Selection strategy

Bet only when the model's predicted gap from the line is 2 points or more. Flat stake is
1 unit. "Sized" stakes the gap divided by 2, capped at 2.5 units, on the same bets.
Totals are priced at -110. Spreads use the best number among the books quoting.

| Rule and price | Record | Units, flat | ROI, flat (95% CI) | Units, sized | Line moved our way |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spread, gap 2+, first line the day before | 5-0 | +4.5 | too few bets | +6.2 | +2.9 points |
| Totals, gap 2+, DraftKings' opening total | 22-13 | +7.0 | +20% (-13%, +47%) | +12.9 | +1.7 points |
| Totals, gap 2+, first total seen on game day | 14-13 | -0.3 | -1% (-36%, +34%) | +1.3 | 0.0 points |

Other cuts of the same games:

- Totals at a 3+ point gap against the opener: 9-1.
- Spread at a 1.5+ point gap: 10-6, +3.1 units.
- Spread games with a gap under 1.5 points, which are not bets: 48-47.
- Totals against the opener by week: 9-7 through October 27, 13-6 after.

### Player points

The first test of the player engine against real lines: 333 graded player-points lines
from 28 games, about 4 hours before tip, 3.8 books per line. We sample only about two
games a night, and the historical lines needed for a backtest are not available free.

- **Log loss:** engine 0.7147, line 0.6904, coin flip 0.6931. The difference is +0.0243
  (95% CI -0.0081, +0.0572). The engine is worse than a coin flip because it is
  overconfident.
- **Average miss:** engine 5.20 points, line 5.02.
- **Price:** the hold is still 4.33% after taking the best over and the best under.

| Engine vs line, in probability | Bets | Hit rate | ROI at the best price (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5+ points | 238 | 53.4% | +2.3% (-9.7%, +14.9%) |
| 10+ points | 151 | 54.3% | +4.2% (-11.2%, +19.4%) |
| 20+ points | 49 | 65.3% | +26.6% (-1.0%, +53.4%) |

Results improve as the disagreement grows, but every interval includes zero. These
thresholds were chosen after the engine had already run, and the best price is the best
among the books quoted at that moment. Not bettable as it stands.

## Rookies

Before the 2026-11-05 fix, every 2026-27 rookie was priced as a league-average player.

- **Coverage:** all 53 rookies who have played now have a rating, covering 100% of rookie
  minutes. Rookies play 6.9% of all minutes.
- **Updating:** the 23 in a rotation (3+ games of 12+ minutes) have a median of 8 games
  and 8 rating updates. Their ratings average -1.61 and have moved 0.80 points on average
  since their first one.
- **Before a first rating:** a default by draft slot, from -1.58 for top-5 picks to -2.43
  for second-round picks.

How far a rating taken two weeks into a season can be trusted, 2022-23 to 2025-26:

| | Players | Correlation with 2 months later | Correlation with season end | Average change by season end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rookies | 225 | 0.51 | 0.32 | +0.56 |
| Veterans | 1,749 | 0.94 | 0.87 | +0.04 |

So rookie ratings stay rough for a while, and they tend to start too low. That matters
most for the few teams that lean heavily on rookies. The rating provider revises its past
values, so these correlations may look somewhat better than what was knowable at the time.

## How to read this

- **The totals result is a clean out-of-sample test** of a frozen model, but 35 bets
  cannot tell 63% from the 56% the backtest suggests, and the ROI interval includes zero.
- **The spread result is five bets**, and these same dates were looked at while the
  day-before rule was being designed. It is an anecdote.
- **The edge is at the opening price and it goes quickly.** On 2026-11-05, two of seven
  next-day totals had moved 3 to 5 points toward the model before a 30-minute poll first
  saw them. No opening total was captured live before that date, so the opening-price
  rows assume a fill that has not been demonstrated. The game-day row is what the system
  could actually have bet, and it is break-even.
- **Log loss and the strategy are measuring different things.** The model does not
  forecast better than the market on average. The strategy only bets the few games where
  the two disagree most, and only at the first price.

Both rules now run as pre-registered paper views that record the price actually on
screen, the opening price, and the close, so the capture question gets a direct answer
as the season goes on. No capital has been deployed.
